The resolution verification layer

One extra step, split into five checkpoints. Every checkpoint is logged, so a case can only move forward with something to show for it.

01

Reported

Citizen files the issue with a photo, geotag, and timestamp attached at the source.

⛓
02

Work done

Department marks the fix complete — this alone can't close the case.

⛓
03

Proof submitted

A before/after photo is uploaded and anchored to a tamper-evident record.

⛓
04

Verification

An automated engine checks time, location, and image difference between before and after.

⛓
05

Resolved

The citizen confirms in person. Only then does the case actually close.
